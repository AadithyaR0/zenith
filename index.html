1. Dual-Layer Storage Engine & ITP Eviction Guard IndexedDB v2 + Mirror
Every log writes transactionally to native IndexedDB (ZenithOfflineVaultDB across 4 object stores: weight_logs, food_logs, habits, user_settings) AND mirrors synchronously to localStorage. On boot, Zenith calls navigator.storage.persist() so iOS WebKit exempts your vault from 7-day Intelligent Tracking Prevention (ITP) cache eviction.
2. Cross-Version Reconciliation (Smart Merge vs Replace) Zero-Loss Merge
Smart Merge (Safe) reconciles backups from any app version without wiping newer logs: • weight_logs: keyed by YYYY-MM-DD (latest timestamp wins) • food_logs: unioned by UUID + sub-ingredient arrays preserved • habits: completedDates & restDates set-unioned & sorted • rollback: pre-restore snapshot saved to zenith_pre_restore_rollback_v2
3. Cryptographic Integrity & Input Sanitization fnv1a-00000000
Incoming JSON payloads pass through a custom JSON.parse reviver that strips prototype-pollution vectors (__proto__, constructor, prototype), enforces a 15 MB DoS ceiling, clamps numeric bounds, and computes a 32-bit FNV-1a integrity hash.
